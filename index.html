About Me

Introduction

My name is Jonathan Roberts. I am an Army AI Officer and AI Engineer specializing in counter-UAS platform architecture and advanced autonomous systems. Driven by a distinct focus on upward mobility and high-stakes problem-solving, my technical expertise centers on applied diffusion models and developing 3D semantic scene graphs for Spatial-Semantics-as-a-Service. I bridge the gap between rigorous engineering and practical deployment, turning state-of-the-art AI capabilities into functional, scalable solutions.

Leveraging an academic foundation in international economics alongside advanced AI Engineering research at CMU, I design systems to solve emerging problems with real-world utility. I want to lead the development of highly reliable AI platforms that integrate into existing security software supply chains and drive the mission forward to serve the warfighter in the defending the United States against the conflicts of the near future.
